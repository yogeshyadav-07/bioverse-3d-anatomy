Human Anatomy 3D Learning — Hindi & English

BioVerse 3D is an educational human anatomy website designed for school students. It helps learners understand the human body, organs and body systems through readable explanations and interactive 3D model viewing.

Human Skeleton 3D Model

Explore the human skeleton to understand bones, skull, spine, rib cage, joints and the role of the skeletal system in support, protection and movement.

Human Heart 3D Model

Learn the basic structure and function of the heart, including its role in pumping blood through the pulmonary and systemic circulation.

Human Lungs 3D Model

Explore lung anatomy and learn how the respiratory system supports breathing and gas exchange between air and blood.

Other Human Body Parts

Students can search for additional anatomy models such as brain, eye, ear, kidney, liver, stomach, intestine, spine and other organs using the Sketchfab search option.

For School Biology Learning

  • Hindi and English language selection.
  • Simple descriptions of body parts and their functions.
  • Real-world learning examples.
  • Interactive 3D viewing for selected anatomy models.
  • Search option for additional human anatomy models.
